                                                                                                             Information for Authors



The Lancet Regional Health – Americas is a new open access journal, part of The Lancet’s global initiative to advocate for health-care
quality and access in all regions of the world. The journal publishes high-quality original research that advocates change in, or
illuminates, clinical practice and health policy in the Americas. We also consider relevant reviews, commentaries, and opinion pieces.
The journal invites submissions that are pertaining to regional health subjects, including but not limited to infectious diseases, non-
communicable diseases, child and adolescent health, maternal and reproductive health, emergency care, health policy, and health
equity.

Manuscript preparation must adhere to relevant reporting standards on EQUATOR network website (Enhancing the Quality and
Transparency of Health Research). Further details on the different sections of The Lancet Regional Health – Americas, and how to submit
to the journal, are provided below. If you require further clarification, the journal’s editorial staff will be pleased to help (americas@
lancet.com).

Manuscripts must be solely the work of the author(s) stated, must not have been previously published elsewhere, and must not be under
consideration by another journal. The Lancet journals are signatories of the Recommendations for the Conduct, Reporting, Editing, and                Recommendations for the
